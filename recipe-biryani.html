🍛 Chicken Biryani

A fragrant, celebratory Indian rice dish

⏱ Prep: 30 min🍳 Cook: 45 min👥 Serves: 5 🔴 Difficulty: Hard🍗 Non-Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Rice:
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 bay leaves, 4 cloves, 2 cardamom
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Chicken:
  • 700g chicken pieces
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 large onions, fried golden
  • 4 tbsp ghee
  • Saffron in warm milk
  • Fresh mint & coriander

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Marinate: Mix chicken with yogurt, biryani masala, turmeric, ginger-garlic paste, salt, and half the fried onions. Marinate for 1 hour or overnight.
2
Parboil rice: Soak rice for 30 min. Boil in salted water with whole spices until 70% cooked. Drain and set aside.
3
Cook chicken: In a heavy pot, heat ghee. Add marinated chicken and cook on medium-high for 10 minutes until masala thickens.
4
Layer: Spread rice over chicken. Drizzle saffron milk. Top with remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, and a spoon of ghee.
5
Dum cook: Seal the pot tightly with foil, then lid. Cook on the lowest heat for 25–30 minutes.
6
Serve: Rest 5 minutes before opening. Mix gently from the sides. Serve hot with raita.
Tip: Never lift the lid during dum cooking. The steam is what finishes the rice and merges all the flavours.
