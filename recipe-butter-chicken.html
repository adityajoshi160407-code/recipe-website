🍗 Butter Chicken

A rich and creamy Indian classic

⏱ Prep: 20 min 🍳 Cook: 25 min 👥 Serves: 4 🟡 Difficulty: Medium 🍗 Non-Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Marinade:
  • 600g chicken, cubed
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Sauce:
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 4 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Marinate: Mix chicken with yogurt, turmeric, chilli powder, ginger-garlic paste, and salt. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
2
Cook chicken: Heat 1 tbsp butter in a pan over high heat. Cook chicken for 6–8 minutes, turning, until slightly charred. Set aside.
3
Make base: In the same pan, melt remaining butter. Sauté onion until golden (7–8 min). Add ginger-garlic paste and cook 1 min.
4
Add spices & tomatoes: Stir in chilli powder, cumin, garam masala. Add tomato puree and simmer 10 minutes.
5
Blend: Let sauce cool, blend until smooth. Strain back into the pan for a silky texture.
6
Finish: Add cream, sugar, and crushed kasuri methi. Add cooked chicken and simmer 5 minutes. Serve with naan or rice!
Tip: Blending and straining the sauce is the secret to restaurant-style butter chicken. Don't skip it!
