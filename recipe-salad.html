🥗 Mediterranean Salad

Fresh, healthy, and ready in 10 minutes

⏱ Prep: 10 min🍳 No Cook👥 Serves: 3 🟢 Difficulty: Easy🥦 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 large cucumbers, diced
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 1 red onion, sliced thin
  • 1 cup kalamata olives
  • 200g feta cheese, cubed
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Fresh mint (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Chop cucumbers, tomatoes, and capsicum into 1–2 cm chunks. Thinly slice the red onion. Add all to a large bowl.
2
Add olives and feta cheese to the bowl.
3
In a small bowl, whisk olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper until combined.
4
Pour dressing over the salad and toss gently so the feta stays intact.
5
Garnish with mint if using. Serve immediately or chill for 15 minutes to let flavours develop.
Tip: Soak the sliced onion in cold water for 10 minutes to make it milder and crunchier.
