👨‍🍳 Cooking Tips

Simple tips to help you cook better every day

Knife Skills


🔪 Keep Your Knife Sharp

A sharp knife is safer than a dull one. A dull blade requires extra force and can slip. Hone your knife before each use and sharpen it every few months.

🧅 Reduce Onion Tears

Chill your onion for 15 minutes before cutting. Cold temperatures slow the release of compounds that irritate your eyes. Always use a very sharp knife.

🧄 Peel Garlic Quickly

Place a clove under the flat side of your knife and press firmly. The skin slips right off — fast and easy.


Heat & Cooking


🔥 Preheat Your Pan

Always preheat the pan before adding oil or food. A hot pan prevents sticking and gives better browning and flavour.

🍳 Don't Crowd the Pan

Too many ingredients at once drops the temperature and causes steaming instead of frying. Cook in batches for best results.

🌡️ Use a Thermometer

Check internal temperatures for meat — 75°C for chicken, 70°C for beef (medium). Guessing leads to over or undercooked food.


Flavour Tips


🧂 Salt at Every Stage

Season as you cook, not just at the end. A pinch of salt when sautéing vegetables, pasta water, and finishing builds deep flavour.

🍋 Use Acid to Brighten Dishes

A squeeze of lemon or a dash of vinegar at the end lifts and brightens any dish without making it taste sour.

🌿 Time Your Herbs Right

Hardy herbs like rosemary and thyme go in early. Delicate herbs like basil and coriander should only be added at the very end or as garnish.


Baking Basics


⚖️ Measure Precisely

Baking is science. Use a kitchen scale for accuracy — especially for flour, which varies a lot depending on how it's packed.

🧈 Use Room Temperature Ingredients

Cold butter and eggs don't mix well and can cause batters to curdle. Take them out 30–60 minutes before baking.

🎂 Don't Open the Oven Early

Opening the door in the first 3/4 of baking time causes temperature drops that can make cakes sink. Wait until the timer goes off.
