🍝 Creamy Pasta Primavera

A comforting vegetarian pasta with fresh vegetables

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🍳 Cook: 15 min 👥 Serves: 4 🟢 Difficulty: Easy 🥦 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 300g penne pasta
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes
  • Fresh basil to garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil salted water and cook pasta until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2
Heat butter and olive oil in a pan. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3
Add broccoli, bell pepper, and zucchini. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until just tender. Season with salt and pepper.
4
Pour in cream, add half the Parmesan and chilli flakes. Simmer on low for 2–3 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5
Add drained pasta and toss well. Add pasta water if needed. Fold in the cherry tomatoes.
6
Serve on plates topped with remaining Parmesan and fresh basil. Enjoy!
Tip: Add a spoon of cream cheese for extra richness. Leftovers reheat well with a splash of milk.
