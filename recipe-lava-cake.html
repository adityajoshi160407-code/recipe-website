🍫 Chocolate Lava Cake

Warm molten chocolate — the ultimate dessert

⏱ Prep: 10 min🍳 Cook: 12 min👥 Serves: 4 🟡 Difficulty: Medium🍰 Dessert

Ingredients

  • 150g dark chocolate (70%)
  • 100g unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 120g icing sugar
  • 60g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter & cocoa for ramekins
  • Vanilla ice cream to serve

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Preheat oven to 200°C. Grease 4 ramekins with butter and dust with cocoa powder.
2
Melt chocolate and butter together over simmering water, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.
3
Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and icing sugar together until pale and slightly thick, about 2 minutes. Add vanilla.
4
Fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture. Sift in flour and salt, fold until just combined — do not overmix.
5
Pour batter evenly into the prepared ramekins. Can be refrigerated up to 24 hours at this point.
6
Bake for 10–12 minutes. Edges should be set but the centre still jiggles. Invert onto plates immediately and serve with ice cream!
Tip: Do a test run with one ramekin first. Timing is everything — 10 min for a very gooey centre, 12 min for firmer.
