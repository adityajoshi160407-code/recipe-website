About Us

The story behind Delicious Recipes

Our Story

Delicious Recipes was created with a simple goal: make great food accessible to everyone. Whether you are cooking for the first time or have years of experience, every recipe here is written to be clear and easy to follow.

This website was built by Aditya Joshi, a student at South Asian University (SAU), as a web development project. It started as a personal collection of favourite home-cooked dishes and grew into a full recipe website.

Every recipe on this site has been tested to make sure the instructions work and the food tastes great. No fancy equipment needed — just basic ingredients and a love of cooking.


What We Offer

  • Tested recipes that actually work
  • Recipes for all dietary preferences
  • Clear, beginner-friendly instructions
  • Helpful cooking tips and techniques
  • Recipes from Indian and global cuisines
