🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Thick, airy pancakes for a perfect breakfast

⏱ Prep: 5 min🍳 Cook: 15 min👥 Serves: 4 🟢 Difficulty: Easy🥦 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter for the pan
  • Maple syrup & berries to serve

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
In a large bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, and salt together.
2
In a separate bowl, whisk buttermilk, milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla until combined.
3
Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined — a few lumps are fine. Do NOT overmix.
4
Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat. Add a knob of butter. Once foamy, ladle about ⅓ cup batter per pancake.
5
Cook until bubbles form on top and edges look set (2–3 min). Flip once and cook 1–2 min more until golden.
6
Stack on warm plates. Serve with maple syrup, fresh berries, and a dusting of icing sugar.
Tip: Rest the batter for 5 minutes before cooking. Buttermilk is key to fluffy texture — don't skip it!
